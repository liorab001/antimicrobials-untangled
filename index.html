It must NEVER be used to diagnose, treat, cure, prevent, or guide prescribing decisions for any real patient.
2. ALGORITHMIC PARSING & SELF-REGULATION LIMITATIONS: Bullet-point summaries, search interpretations, pathogen-disease filters, renal dosage highlights, and interaction badges are generated automatically by experimental client-side text-parsing heuristics.
